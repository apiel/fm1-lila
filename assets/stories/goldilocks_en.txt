# GOLDILOCKS AND THE THREE BEARS (en): a retelling of the traditional tale (public domain). One line per key (27), in order. A line: SCENE|text
# SCENE: FOREST HOUSE KITCHEN CHAIRS BEDROOM, + "*" when the three bears are there too
FOREST|Once upon a time, there was a little girl with golden hair. Her name was Goldilocks.
FOREST|One sunny morning, Goldilocks went for a walk in the forest.
HOUSE|Deep in the woods, she found a little house. The door was open.
HOUSE|Hello? Is anybody home? she called. But nobody answered.
HOUSE*|In that house lived three bears. Papa Bear, Mama Bear, and Baby Bear.
HOUSE*|They had gone for a walk, while their porridge cooled down.
KITCHEN|On the table, Goldilocks saw three bowls of porridge.
KITCHEN|She tasted the big bowl. Ouch! This porridge is too hot!
KITCHEN|She tasted the middle bowl. Brr! This porridge is too cold!
KITCHEN|She tasted the little bowl. Mmm! This porridge is just right! And she ate it all up.
CHAIRS|Then Goldilocks saw three chairs.
CHAIRS|The big chair was too hard. The middle chair was too soft.
CHAIRS|The little chair was just right. But crack! It broke into pieces!
BEDROOM|Goldilocks felt very tired, so she went upstairs.
BEDROOM|There were three beds. The big bed was too high.
BEDROOM|The middle bed was too low.
BEDROOM|The little bed was just right. And Goldilocks fell fast asleep.
FOREST*|Soon, the three bears came home from their walk.
KITCHEN*|Somebody has been eating my porridge! growled Papa Bear.
KITCHEN*|Somebody has been eating my porridge! said Mama Bear.
KITCHEN*|Somebody has been eating my porridge, and they ate it all up! cried Baby Bear.
CHAIRS*|Somebody has been sitting in my chair, and they broke it! cried Baby Bear.
BEDROOM*|The three bears tiptoed upstairs, very, very quietly.
BEDROOM*|Somebody has been sleeping in my bed, and she is still there! cried Baby Bear.
BEDROOM*|Goldilocks woke up and saw the three bears. Oh! she screamed.
FOREST|She jumped out of the window and ran all the way home.
FOREST|And she never went back to the house of the three bears. The end!
