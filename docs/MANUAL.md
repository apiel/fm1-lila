# LILA: the manual

LILA is a synth for a very young child. Everything on the panel does one thing, the four knobs under the screen always do
something, and **whatever you turn, the picture changes with it**.

## The rules

- **The keys play.** Nothing else. `OCT-` and `OCT+` change the octave (a banner says which).
- **Every button has one page.** Press it and the four knobs under the screen (KNOB 1 to 4) become what the strip at the bottom of
  the screen says. The strip slides away after a few seconds so the world has the whole screen; touch anything and it comes back.
- **A lit button** is the page you are on. A faintly lit button is a page you can go to.
- Nothing starts by itself: the beat, the arpeggiator and the loop are off when the FM-1 starts.

## The big knobs

| Knob | What it does |
|---|---|
| **MASTER** | the volume (the top of the knob is 80 % of full: it is a toy for small ears) |
| **PRESETS** | the world. The picture closes like a camera iris and opens on the next world, with its own sound |
| **ALGORITHM** | the **mood**: SUNNY, NIGHT (darker, softer, dreamy), MAGIC (sparkly, echoing), CRAZY (wobbly, crunchy, the colours spin) |
| **SELECT** | the song, **while a song plays**. Stopped, it only reminds you to press PLAY |

## The pages

This is the **generic** layout. Each world then takes over some of these knobs and gives them its own names and meanings: see "Each world's own knobs" below. (FX, SEL, ENV and LFO mostly stay as they are; HOME and EDIT are where a world is itself.)

| Button | Page | KNOB 1 | KNOB 2 | KNOB 3 | KNOB 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HOME** | MAGIC | **BEND**: slides the pitch up or down (the animal leans and floats) | **STARS**: fairy dust after every note, stars in the sky | **SIZE**: tiny to giant: the animal grows and the sound gets deeper | **WIGGLE**: a drunken pitch and a jelly picture |
| **FX** | FX | **ECHO**: ghosts of the animal repeat | **SPACE**: a big room; rings spread over the world | **FLUFFY**: a shimmering double (chorus); each world has its own kind: a doubler (cow, blocks), a flanger (meteor), a chorus for the wolf's pack, a phaser (frog, space), an auto-pan (jungle), a ring modulator (robot), fuzz (dinosaur) | **ROBOT**: crunchy sound, the whole world turns to big square pixels |
| **SEL** | FILTER | **BRIGHT**: dark and muffled to bright and sunny (the colours follow) | **WOW**: a ringing, whistling filter: sparkles | **TYPE**: SOFT (warm), TALKY, THIN (cool), HOLLOW, HONK, VOCAL; every world starts with its own | **GRIT**: rough and dirty; static on the picture |
| **ENV** | ENVELOPE | **FADE IN**: how slowly a note arrives (the animal sings in slowly) | **PLUCK**: from an organ that holds, to a pluck that dies at once | **FADE OUT**: how long it rings after you let go | **BOING**: the filter jumps open on each note; the animal squashes and stretches |
| **LFO** | WOBBLE | **SPEED** | **WOBBLE**: how much | **WHAT**: SIREN (the pitch), WAH (the brightness), TREMBLE (the volume): the animal bobs, flashes or pulses | **WAVE**: SMOOTH, ZIGZAG, CHOPPY, BUMPY |
| **EDIT** | SOUND | **SHAPE**: sine, triangle, saw, square: the notes that float up change shape too | **BUDDY**: a twin voice a little out of tune: a twin animal stands behind | **BIG**: a deep octave underneath; the ground shakes | **METAL**: bell and robot tones; the colours go silver |
| **GLO** | PARTY | **TEMPO** of the beat, the arpeggio, the songs and the loop | **KEY**: up or down by up to 6 semitones; the colours turn | **EASY**: ANY note, PENTA or MAJOR: only notes that always sound right (the keys of the scale light faintly) | **FRIENDS**: ALONE, TWINS (octave), POWER (fifth), FAMILY (a chord): friends of the animal appear beside it |
| **ARP** | ARPEGGIO | **MODE**: OFF, UP, DOWN, UP-DOWN, RANDOM | **SPEED**: SNAIL, TURTLE, RABBIT, CHEETAH | **RANGE**: 1, 2 or 3 octaves | **STICKY**: YES keeps the notes going after you let go |
| **SEQ** | BEAT | **STYLE**: OFF, MARCH, ROCK, DISCO, REGGAE, HIP HOP, POLKA, ROBOT, PARTY | **LOUD** | **BOUNCE**: from straight to swingy | **TUNING**: the drums from deep to tiny |
| **PLAY** | SONG | **SPEED** (the same as TEMPO) | **MODE**: LISTEN, or FOLLOW: the song waits and the next key lights up | **REPEAT**: ONCE, REPEAT, or ALL (one song after another) | **BAND**: SOLO, or ZOO: every note is a different animal |
| **REC** | LOOP | **LENGTH**: 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars | **LOUD** | **SPEED**: HALF (slow and low), NORMAL, DOUBLE (fast, a chipmunk) | **FUNNY**: moves the whole loop up or down by up to 12 semitones |

### Buttons that do something when you press them

- **SAVE**: has no page of its own; pressed **together with GLO** it changes the screen (see below).
- **ARP**: turns the arpeggiator on (UP) and shows its page. Press ARP again to turn it off. Hold some keys: they play one after another.
- **SEQ**: starts the MARCH beat and shows its page. Press SEQ again to stop it. The drum pads on the screen light up as they hit.
- **PLAY / STOP**: plays the current song in the current world; press again to stop. The beat (if on) starts with it. While it plays,
  turn **SELECT** for another song. In FOLLOW the key to press blinks; any octave of the right note counts.
- **REC**: the first press arms it (a red dot blinks, the button blinks); the first key you play starts the recording. When the loop is full
  it plays back by itself (press REC to finish early: the loop is as long as what you played, rounded up to the beat). Press REC once more
  to throw the loop away. You can change the world while it plays: the loop plays your tune with the new sound.
- **OCT- / OCT+**: the keyboard's octave. Hold both for five seconds to put the FM-1 in update mode (parents).

## The piano worlds and the instruments

After the jungle: **PIANO** (a concert hall with red curtains), **E-PIANO** (a neon synthwave night) and **ORGAN** (a cathedral with a rose
window and glowing pipes). The panel's 27 keys are drawn as a keyboard at the bottom of the screen: every key sinks and lights in its own
colour when it sounds (pressed, or played by a song, the arpeggiator or the loop) and sends a coloured note rising. A little singing note
floats above and takes everything the other animals take (size, boing, twins, echo ghosts, a shimmering double). Their knobs have piano
names: HALL / ROOM / CHORUS (or PHASER, ROTARY), HAMMER and PEDAL, STRINGS, BASS, SHINE.

Then five more instruments, each with its own picture and its own knobs; the keys light up in all of them:

| World | Sound | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| **XYLO** | TING: wooden bars with a bright mallet click (MALLET, RING, WOOD, METAL) | a bear hitting a rainbow xylophone, the bars jump |
| **HARP** | PLUCK: a long ringing string, shimmer and angels (RING, SHIMMER, SPARKLE) | a fairy and a golden harp: each string glows and shivers (C strings red, F blue) |
| **UKULELE** | STRUM: every key strums a chord, down, then up (STRUM = how slowly, PICK, TWANG) | a crab strumming on a beach at sunset |
| **FLUTE** | TOOT: a breathy tone with a little chiff and a late vibrato (BREATH, CHIFF, VIBRATO, BLOW) | a snake rising from its basket, the flute's holes light |
| **KALIMBA** | TINK: a thumb piano, short metallic tines with a little shimmer (THUMB, RING, TINES, BUZZ) | a meerkat playing a kalimba on the savanna at sunset |

## The screen: all, a name, none

**SAVE + GLO pressed together** change the screen (a lone press of GLO still opens its page, a moment later; SAVE alone only shows a hint):

1. **all of it**, as it is;
2. **a name**: the screen stays black and shows, for one second, the world you chose, the beat you chose or the song you chose;
3. **none**: the backlight is off. The lights of the keys and buttons still tell what happens.

The choice is kept after the FM-1 is switched off.

## The five story worlds

After the instruments, five stories in French, each with its own pictures: **CORBEAU** (*Le Corbeau et le Renard*, in La Fontaine's own words: a raven with his cheese and a fox), **BOEUF** (*La Grenouille qui voulait se faire aussi grosse que le Bœuf*: a frog that swells next to an ox), **LION** (*Le Lion et le Rat*), **BOUCLE** (*Boucle d'or et les trois ours*) and **CHAPERON** (*Le Petit Chaperon rouge*: the girl in her hood, the wolf, the grandmother's house and bed).

- **Each key tells one part of the story**, in order from the lowest key (up to 27 parts). A story of 16 parts or fewer uses **the white keys only**: the black keys stay silent and only the white keys that have a part glow (a fable has nine parts, Little Red Riding Hood twelve; Goldilocks has 27 and uses every key). The keys that have a part glow faintly, the part being
  told is lit, and its words are written at the top of the screen for whoever is reading along.
- **PLAY** tells the whole story, part after part; PLAY again stops. While it plays, **SELECT** goes to the part before or after.
- The voice is made like the old talking toys (LPC speech) and **played by the synth**, so everything still works on it:

| Page | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HOME | **SPEED** (slower or faster, the pitch stays) | **ROBOT** (the voice goes flat, like a robot) | **SIZE** (a mouse's voice to a giant's) | **PITCH** |
| EDIT | **VOICE** (CLASSIC: the old talking toy; or the synth's sine, triangle, saw, square) | **CHOIR** (a second voice, out of tune) | **BIG** (a deep voice underneath) | **WHISPER** |
| PLAY | **SPEED** | **ECHO** (the story ends and stops: it never starts again) | **PAUSE** (between the parts) | **CAVE** (reverb) |

FX (echo, space, chorus, robot), SEL (the filter), ENV (fade in, fade out), LFO (a wobbling voice), GLO's KEY and FRIENDS (the voice sings
in chords), the octave buttons, the beat, and REC (record which parts you press, and loop them) all work on the story too.

The voices are made on a computer with Piper, free text-to-speech: LJ Speech for English (public domain) and SIWIS for French
(CC BY 4.0). See BUILDING.md to tell a story of your own.

## For grown-ups

- **Playing on a computer or keyboard:** USB MIDI notes from a keyboard play the sound directly (no octave, easy scale or key).
- **Calibration:** if a button or knob does the wrong thing, hold OCT- and OCT+ while you switch the FM-1 on, and press / turn what the screen asks for.
- **Update mode:** hold OCT- and OCT+ for five seconds. A countdown appears; let go before it ends to cancel.
- **Crackling:** the audio keeps a measure of its own load and takes voices away (down to five) if it runs out of time, so it should degrade
  into fewer notes at once rather than crackle.
- **After a crash:** a red LILA CRASH screen, then a restart by itself. After two crashes while starting, a safe mode with no sound and USB on,
  so the firmware can be reinstalled; PLAY tries again.
